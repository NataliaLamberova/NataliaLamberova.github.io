# Website final review

## Positioning

The revised site presents Natalia Lamberova as an applied political economist whose work connects political institutions, public preferences, information, and technological change. The public-facing structure now separates published scholarship, broader research programs, teaching, and institution-building work.

## Three-reader critique

### Hiring committee

The strongest signal is now the coherent research agenda: the homepage leads with a clear intellectual identity, the publications page contains only completed scholarly work, and the research page shows how individual projects form three cumulative programs. The remaining weakness is that several working projects cannot yet demonstrate external validation. Once those papers are ready for circulation, add a selective working-paper section with abstracts, dates, and stable PDFs rather than expanding the site with every early project.

### Prospective PhD student

The Origins of Ideas Research Lab and the teaching page now make mentorship, research design, and hands-on analytical training visible. The Policy Lab description also identifies a concrete student deliverable without disclosing the confidential partner or policy setting. A future improvement would be a short “Working with students” section describing how students join research projects, what preparation is useful, and whether funded RA opportunities are offered.

### Potential collaborator

The research-program organization makes substantive overlap easier to identify, and Research Beyond Publications demonstrates capacity to organize teams and translate research into institutional and policy work. Collaboration would be even easier if the site later added one sentence per active public project naming the desired complementary expertise or data partnership.
